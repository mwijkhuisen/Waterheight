# Permission tracker (C1–C36)

Owner actions C1–C13 of issue #14 and C14–C36 of issue #62 (PHASES §6.2 C): C14 (RWS, wider scope and request budget), C15 (Het Waterschapshuis and the Unie van Waterschappen) and C16–C36 (one request per water board). The e-mail drafts are in [`docs/legal/requests/`](legal/requests/README.md). **Every consent request** (C1–C6, C11, C12, the C13 part of the BAFU e-mail, the C10 templates and the C16–C36 water-board requests) asks whether (a) machine-readable redistribution through our public API and exports is allowed and (b) we may keep and republish a history archive (catalogue §0.2, §0.7). C7 and C14 (RWS), C8 (ITZBund/WSV), the second C9 e-mail (BAFU history orders) and the LANUK notice are courtesy notices about open-licence sources and do not ask them; C15 is a question about a national route.

How to use it: when you send an e-mail, fill in *Sent* and a *Go/no-go* date (the date after which the fallback ships, catalogue §0.2). When an answer arrives, fill in *Answered*, *Outcome*, *Conditions* and the *Channels granted* (`display`, `api`, `bulk_export`, `history_export`; channels a permission does not name stay off). A grant becomes `registry/permissions/<ID>.md` in a P13 PR (P12 for DE-2), whose front matter states the grant (see `CLAUDE.md`, "Audience rules"); CI requires it before an audience or channel changes. Keep the original e-mails in your own mail archive, never in this public repository, and write no names or addresses of provider staff here.
